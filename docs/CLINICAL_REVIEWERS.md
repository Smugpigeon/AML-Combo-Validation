## Sign-off process

To be added as a reviewer:

1. Submit a PR adding your entry below in this format:
   ```markdown
   ### Dr. [Family Name], [Given Name] MD/PhD
   - **Credential**: [board certification, e.g., "Hematology, PUMC"]
   - **Institution**: [hospital/university]
   - **Email**: [contact]
   - **Date added**: YYYY-MM-DD
   - **Scope**: [which sections of CLINICAL_REVIEW.md you commit to reviewing]
   ```
2. After your PR merges, claim sign-off rows in `CLINICAL_REVIEW.md` by
   editing each ⚠ / ⭐ rule individually with your name + date.
3. If you request rule modifications during review, file them as separate
   PRs and link them in your sign-off footnote.

## Conflicts of interest

Reviewers must disclose any of:
- Speaker / advisory board fees from regimen sponsors (Astellas / AbbVie /
  Bristol Myers Squibb / Daiichi Sankyo / Servier)
- Equity / consulting in AML diagnostics or therapeutics companies
- PI / co-author on any of the cited trials (this is welcome, just disclose)

Disclosures appear next to your roster entry.
